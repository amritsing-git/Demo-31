How can I place an order?

Send us a message on Instagram or WhatsApp with the treat you would like, your preferred date and any design or flavour ideas.

Absolutely. Share your occasion, theme, preferred size and flavour so we can discuss the details with you.

Where can I see prices?

Prices depend on the item and customisation. Message us for the latest menu and a quote before confirming your order.

Yes. For paid collaborations and promotions, please get in touch through our social page.
